# Financial Planning Worksheet (FPW)

FPW is an offline, privacy-focused personal financial planning tool designed to help users organize income, expenses, debts, savings goals, net worth, and debt payoff strategies.

## Feedback & Support

Have a suggestion, found a bug, or have an idea that could make FPW better? Feedback is always welcome.

**Email:** devilcollapse@gmail.com

## Privacy

FPW is designed to keep your financial information on your device. No FPW account or bank connection is required.

[View the FPW Privacy Policy](https://devilcollapse.github.io/fpw/privacy)
