# Privacy Policy for Financial Planning Worksheet (FPW)

**Effective Date:** September 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 14, 2026

Financial Planning Worksheet ("FPW") is a financial planning and budgeting application developed and published by **DEVILCOLLAPSE**.

FPW is designed with privacy as a core feature. The application's primary financial planning features operate locally on the user's device and do not require an FPW account or an FPW-operated cloud service.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use FPW.

---

## 1. Information You Enter Into FPW

FPW allows you to voluntarily enter information for financial planning purposes, including:

- Income and deductions
- Expenses
- Assets and liabilities
- Debt balances
- Interest rates and payment information
- Savings goals
- Net worth information
- Financial planning projections
- Financial notes
- Other financial planning information you choose to enter

FPW does not require you to create an FPW account to use these features.

Information entered into FPW's financial planning features is intended to remain stored locally on your device unless you intentionally export, share, copy, or otherwise transmit it outside the application.

DEVILCOLLAPSE does not operate an FPW cloud database for storing your financial worksheet data.

---

## 2. Financial Notes

FPW includes a Financial Notes feature for storing private notes.

Financial Notes use secure storage capabilities provided by the device and operating system and may require device authentication before they can be accessed.

FPW does not intentionally transmit the contents of Financial Notes to DEVILCOLLAPSE or to an FPW-operated server.

Financial Notes are not included in FPW Excel exports.

Although Financial Notes are designed for private financial planning information, users should not use them as a password manager or credential vault. Users should avoid storing passwords, authentication codes, full Social Security numbers, full financial account numbers, private cryptographic keys, or similar authentication credentials.

---

## 3. Biometric and Device Authentication

FPW may use authentication services provided by your device or operating system to protect access to the application or certain features.

Depending on the device, authentication may include:

- Fingerprint recognition
- Facial recognition
- Device PIN
- Password
- Passcode
- Other device-supported authentication methods

Biometric verification is performed by the device or operating system.

FPW does not receive or store your fingerprint, facial scan, biometric template, or other raw biometric information.

FPW receives only the authentication result or related information necessary to determine whether access should be permitted.

---

## 4. Local Storage

FPW is designed primarily as an offline application.

Financial planning information is stored locally using storage mechanisms available on the user's device.

Some information requiring additional protection, such as Financial Notes, may use secure storage facilities provided by the operating system.

The behavior of device backups, cloud backups, device transfers, operating-system restoration, and application restoration may depend on the operating system, device manufacturer, platform settings, and services selected by the user.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for their devices and device accounts.

---

## 5. Excel Exports and Sharing

FPW allows users to export financial planning information to an Excel workbook.

An export is created only when initiated by the user.

FPW may temporarily create files necessary to generate and share an exported workbook. FPW is designed to remove temporary export files after a limited retention period.

Once an exported file is intentionally saved, copied, uploaded, emailed, messaged, transmitted, or shared with another person, application, cloud provider, storage provider, or service, that copy is outside FPW's control.

The recipient or third-party service may retain or process the exported information according to its own policies.

Users are responsible for protecting and deleting copies of exported information stored outside FPW.

**Financial Notes are not included in FPW Excel exports.**

---

## 6. Information Collected by DEVILCOLLAPSE

FPW does not require an FPW user account and does not operate an FPW cloud service for storing users' financial worksheets.

DEVILCOLLAPSE does not intentionally collect or receive the financial information that users enter into FPW's local financial planning features.

If FPW's data practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial planning information stored by FPW generally remains on the user's device until it is:

- Changed by the user
- Deleted by the user
- Removed using FPW's data-erasure functionality
- Removed through application or device storage management
- Removed when application data is deleted, subject to operating-system behavior

FPW provides functionality for erasing application data.

Temporary files created by FPW for export and sharing operations are designed to be removed automatically after a limited period.

Files that users intentionally save, send, upload, copy, or share outside FPW must be managed or deleted separately.

Uninstalling FPW may remove locally stored application information, but backup or restoration behavior can vary depending on the operating system, device settings, and platform services.

Users should preserve any information they wish to keep before erasing application data, resetting a device, or uninstalling FPW.
